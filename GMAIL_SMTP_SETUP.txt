GMAIL SMTP SETUP FOR VIOLETTE TRAIN
=====================================
Set up: May 4, 2026

STEP 1: Enable 2-Factor Authentication
------------------------------------
1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com
2. Click "Security" in the left sidebar
3. Find "2-Step Verification" and turn it ON
4. Follow the prompts to verify your phone number

STEP 2: Generate App Password
----------------------------
1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
2. Sign in if prompted
3. Select app: "Mail"
4. Select device: "Other (Custom name)" → type "Violette Train"
5. Google will show a 16-character password (with spaces)
   Example: "abcd efgh ijkl mnop"
   COPY THIS — you won't see it again

STEP 3: Add to .env.local
--------------------------
Add these 5 lines to your .env.local file:

SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_SECURE=false
SMTP_USER=YOUR_GMAIL_ADDRESS@gmail.com
SMTP_PASS=YOUR_16_CHAR_APP_PASSWORD

IMPORTANT: Replace "YOUR_GMAIL_ADDRESS" and "YOUR_16_CHAR_APP_PASSWORD"
with your actual values. The password has no spaces when you paste it.

STEP 4: Verify It Works
------------------------
1. Save the .env.local file
2. Restart your dev server: npm run dev
3. Open your website and go to: /vi/admin/settings
4. Log in with your admin token
5. Scroll to "Test Email Configuration"
6. Enter your personal email address (the Gmail one above)
7. Click "Test Email"
8. Check your inbox — you should receive a "Email Configuration Verified" email

TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------
- "Authentication failed": Wrong password — double-check your App Password
- "Connection refused": Gmail may be blocking — try https://accounts.google.com
  and enable "Less secure app access" (or use App Password which you already did)
- Didn't receive email: Check spam/junk folder

LIMITATIONS
-----------
- Gmail free accounts: 500 emails/day max
- App Passwords only work with your Gmail account — can't send from
  a different domain like noreply@violettetrain.vn
